# Code of Conduct

Open Finance Toolkit is for everyone who wants to work with financial data, whatever their background, experience, country or language. We want it to be a friendly, respectful place to learn and build.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Give feedback that helps the other person improve.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect privacy. Never share anyone's personal or financial details.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.

## Who this applies to

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project.

## What happens if it is broken

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, or block people who break these rules. We will try to be fair and to explain our decision.

## How to report a problem

Message the maintainer, [@Itz-https](https://github.com/Itz-https), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. If you prefer, open an issue titled "Conduct concern" without naming anyone, and we will arrange a private conversation. Reports are handled with care and kept private as far as possible.

This code of conduct is a short, plain-language statement inspired by the widely used community standards in open source.
